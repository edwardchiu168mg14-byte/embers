## Binding semantics decided here (supersede PHASE1_GOAL where they differ)

- **Protection is a subtree property.** A protected process (any `ProtectReason`, including `Allowlisted`) protects **all of its descendants**: the BFS used by `tree()`/`descendants()` must not traverse into or beyond a protected node, and no descendant of a protected node may appear in any actionable ember's `pids` — even if that descendant would be an actionable root on its own (it is not; it belongs to something we leave alone). A protected process that is itself a hog is still reported as `Inform`.
- **Merged action = most conservative hit.** Order `Inform > QuitApp > Terminate > TerminateGroup`; additionally `root.is_system_path` ⇒ `Inform` regardless of hits (so E2/E3 alone on a system-path process is also `Inform` — answers a9b).
- **Process-group termination is valid only when** `pgid > 1` **and** every member of the group is either the root or one of its (unprotected) descendants; otherwise fall back to `Terminate` on the tree. Groups containing a protected member or an unrelated process never get `TerminateGroup`.
- **Everything the engine formats is untrusted** (S10): `cwd`, `session_id`, `name`, `exe`, every argv element and the joined hint are redacted and capped — fields at 2048 bytes, `recovery_hint` and `reason` at 2048 bytes total — with the cap applied *after* redaction and with `…` on truncation.
